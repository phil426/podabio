## Full Description

### What is PodaBio?

PodaBio is a comprehensive link-in-bio platform designed exclusively for podcasters and audio content creators. Unlike generic link-in-bio tools, PodaBio understands the unique needs of podcasters, offering features that make sharing, managing, and growing your podcast effortless.

### Core Value Proposition

**One beautiful page. All your links, episodes, and resources. Automatically synced from your RSS feed. Built specifically for podcasters.**

PodaBio eliminates the manual work of managing a link-in-bio page by automatically importing your podcast information, episodes, and artwork directly from your RSS feed. When you publish a new episode, your PodaBio page updates automatically—no manual intervention required.

### Key Features

**🎙️ Podcast-First Features**
- **RSS Feed Auto-Sync**: Automatically imports your podcast name, description, cover art, and episodes from your RSS feed
- **Built-in Podcast Player**: Native web audio player powered by Shikwasa.js, allowing visitors to listen to episodes directly on your page
- **Episode Management**: Automatic episode updates with titles, descriptions, and publish dates
- **Podcast Directory Links**: Quick links to all major podcast platforms (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Google Podcasts, and more)

**🎨 Complete Customization**
- **49+ Professional Themes**: Pre-designed themes optimized for podcast content
- **Custom Theme Builder**: Full control over colors, fonts, and layout
- **15+ Google Fonts**: Choose from professional typography options
- **Custom Domains**: Use your own domain name for a professional brand presence
- **Profile & Background Images**: Upload custom images to match your brand

**📊 Analytics & Insights**
- **Page Views Tracking**: See how many people visit your page
- **Link Click Analytics**: Track which links and episodes get the most engagement
- **Episode Performance**: Understand which episodes drive the most traffic
- **Referrer Data**: See where your visitors are coming from
- **Device & Browser Analytics**: Know how your audience is accessing your page

**🔗 Link Management**
- **Drag-and-Drop Reordering**: Easily organize your links with intuitive drag-and-drop
- **Unlimited Links** (Pro plan): Add as many links as you need
- **Social Media Integration**: Quick links to all major social platforms
- **Custom Links**: Add any URL you want to share

**📧 Email Subscription**
- **Integrated Subscription Forms**: Grow your email list with beautiful drawer-style forms
- **6 Provider Integrations**: Works with Mailchimp, Constant Contact, ConvertKit, AWeber, MailerLite, and Brevo
- **Double Opt-in Support**: Comply with email marketing best practices
- **Subscription Analytics**: Track your email list growth

**🚀 Advanced Features**
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Secure your account with TOTP or email-based 2FA
- **Media Library**: Centralized management for all your uploaded images
- **Custom Widgets**: 20+ widget types for displaying different types of content
- **Mobile-Optimized**: Perfect viewing experience on all devices
- **SEO-Friendly**: Optimized for search engines and social media sharing
